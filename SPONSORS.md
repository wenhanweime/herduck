## 商业合作 / Business cooperation

AGPL-3.0-or-later already covers most organizational use: you may run Herduck inside your
company, on your own machines, and modify it as you see fit, at no cost.

If you need something beyond that, email us. Typical starting points for a conversation:

- **Commercial or embedding questions** — whether AGPL applies to what you have in mind.
- **Rollout, integration, or adaptation** to a team or a specific environment.
- **Custom development**, or a feature your team needs.
- **A support arrangement**, with expectations and scope agreed in writing before work starts.
- **Partnership or product conversations**, including the Chinese market.

This is not a price list and not a service-level commitment. Each engagement is agreed
individually, in writing, before it begins.

## Honest boundaries

- Herduck is in **Alpha**: there is no long-term support release and no guaranteed response
  time. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Questions about **Herdr** itself — its licensing, support, or roadmap — go to Herdr, not here.
- We will not promise a capability we cannot back. If something is out of scope, the first
  reply will say so.
